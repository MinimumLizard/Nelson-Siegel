quote = the day's indicative mid, which is a midpoint of the PDMO's AVERAGE
buying and selling prices across dealers — not a level anyone must deal on
traded = where it last actually changed hands, and how long ago. Executed
levels have been running well above the screen; the bias line at the top
measures that across the market.
resid = distance from the fitted curve, bp (positive = cheap) | gap = from its OWN
recent norm | z = that in its own standard deviations | cost = half the bid-offer
on each leg | exp = historical 10-day reversion at this z
carry = entry yield less funding, bp p.a. | roll = price gain from ageing down
the curve | per dur = the two together per year of duration, which is the only
one of them that is not mostly a bet on duration
Rs bn / days = turnover and days traded over the 60 days to 2026-09-21 — the last day
whose trade file has published. The quote columns run to 2026-09-21; the two feeds
publish on different clocks.
auction = days since it was last sold | * = inside the 14-day post-auction window
