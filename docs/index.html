carry is the entry yield less the cost of funding and roll the price gain from ageing down the curve, both bp per year; per dur is the two together per year of duration. Read that column, not the other two: across this book carry plus roll correlates +0.995 with duration, so ranking on it just ranks by maturity. Per unit of duration the ordering reverses, and today the whole book sits within a few bp of the same number — the curve is charging about the same for every year of risk on it. Carry is quoted at a realistic entry rather than the screen: over the last 20 days trades printed 6bp cheaper than the quote mid. That shift is the same for every bond, so it moves the level of carry and not the ordering.

Rs bn and days are turnover and days traded over the 60 days to 2026-11-11, the last day whose trade file has published — the quote columns run to 2026-11-12, because the two feeds publish on different clocks · auction is days since this bond was last sold, highlighted inside the 14-day window in which freshly auctioned paper has sat about 5bp cheap to its own norm · cover is bids over the amount offered at that auction, so how much demand the last supply met.

Curve shape — how steep, against its own history

The yield at each maturity less the yield at 2 years — the same idea as "2s10s", the usual one-number summary of how much more the market asks to lend for longer. Rising lines mean the curve is steepening. Each point is the fitted curve plus what the bonds near that maturity are actually quoting, so a kink the curve cannot bend to follow still shows.

Context, not a signal. Tested as a trade — enter the day after a spread sits 1.5 sd from its own norm — only 2s5s came back clearly more than chance would give, and by less than crossing the bid-offer on both legs costs. Read it as where the curve's steepness sits against its own recent range, for decisions made on other grounds. python -m signals.validate reprints the test as history grows.

A bond's raw distance from the curve is not a signal: some bonds sit permanently cheap. Each is scored against its own trailing 60-day window, which excludes the day being scored. Measured over this sample the residuals mean-revert with a half-life near 6 days; run python -m signals.validate for the current numbers. One 9-month sample in one regime — evidence the mechanism works, not a forecast of what it pays.
