Most candidates do not clear their own costs as a standalone round trip — the honest use is choosing between trades you were doing anyway.

A bond's raw distance from the curve is not a signal: some bonds sit permanently cheap. Each is scored against its own trailing 60-day window, which excludes the day being scored.
